Kiro Atelier wallpapers
=======================

These 22 wallpapers are 22 different pictures, each painted by Kiro Atelier: Dreamscape,
Palette Knife, Art Deco, Shadow Box, Psychedelic, Layered Paper, Paper Quilling, Origami
and Monsoon. Each file name ends with the style and the strength: how far the painting
moves away from the original picture (0.65 stays closer, 0.75 repaints boldly).

Kiro Atelier turns your own photos and wallpapers into paintings, in 4K, on your own
computer: no cloud, no account, no cost per picture. Drop one picture or a whole folder
on it and pick a style: oil impasto, palette knife, watercolour, pastel, Japanese
woodblock, linocut, charcoal, paper craft, or a style you describe yourself.

Can your PC run it? Check first, it is free and changes nothing:
    sudo pacman -S kiro-atelier-check

Get Kiro Atelier: https://ko-fi.com/s/6ff5175237

The original pictures are wallpapers found on public wallpaper sites; their artists are
unknown. If one is your work, open an issue at
https://github.com/kirodubes/kiro-atelier-wallpapers and we will credit you or remove it.
